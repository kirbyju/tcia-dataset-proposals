# Proposal package

## Expected package contents

The TCIA Dataset Proposal Form can generate a ZIP containing:

- a single-row TSV with form responses;
- a DOCX summary of the same responses;
- a PDF containing the Data Submission Agreement and proposal description; and
- uploaded preprints or manuscripts.

Review the proposal TSV or DOCX and relevant supporting materials. Prefer the structured TSV for field extraction, the DOCX for human-readable labels, and supporting publications for scientific context.

## Data Submission Agreement

Ignore the DSA PDF when providing proposal feedback. TCIA sends the agreement early so the submitting site's legal team can begin reviewing it. Sites are not expected to complete or sign it until after the AG approves the proposal.

Do not inspect, summarize, cite, or evaluate the DSA. Do not flag blank organization fields, notices, signatures, dates, or an incomplete Exhibit A. Do not ask the submitter to execute the agreement before AG review. The DSA's presence or completion status is not evidence for or against the proposal's scientific merit or readiness for AG review.

## Shared proposal information

- Scientific, technical, and legal points of contact
- Full title, short title, authors, and abstract
- Approximate subject count and disk space
- Related manuscripts or preprints
- Previous publication or hosting
- Time constraints, funding, acknowledgements, and reason for using TCIA
- Related software, notebooks, websites, or source code

Contact information is personal data. Confirm that required roles are identified, but do not repeat names, email addresses, or phone numbers in a review unless requested.

## New Collection information

Look for primary disease site, diagnosis, image types, supporting-data types, file formats, preparatory modifications, and whether facial images may be present.

## Analysis Result information

Look for the source TCIA Collection or Collections, derived-data types, file formats, creation or analysis methods, and linkage back to the source images.

## Source precedence

Do not treat the form as the only source. A preprint, publication, data dictionary, protocol, README, or repository may answer a form-level gap. Cite that evidence and avoid asking the PI to duplicate it.
