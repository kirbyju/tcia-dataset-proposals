# NCTN and NCORP proposal routing

Apply this check when the data come from an NCTN or NCORP trial or another cooperative-group study.

## Submitter-initiated proposal

Before asking the AG to review scientific value, verify that the submitter has authority to propose sharing the specified data. Ask whether approval has been obtained from the relevant study leadership, including the study PI, cooperative-group chair, group statistician, and any company or sponsor contacts whose agreements affect data sharing. Ask whether any binding agreements have data-sharing implications.

If authority has not been established, recommend resolving it before AG review. Do not ask the AG to spend time reviewing a proposal that may not be authorized for sharing.

Do not request that approval letters or agreements be pasted into an ordinary review response. Confirm their existence and route them through the appropriate secure or official process.

## CIP-directed publication

When Cancer Imaging Program leadership has directed publication of an NCTN trial, CIP approval is sufficient. The AG may be informed as a courtesy, but no AG vote is required. Do not characterize the courtesy notification as an approval review.

CIP-directed trials may rely on ClinicalTrials.gov, related publications, and linked NCI clinical-data repositories rather than a submitter-completed proposal form or CICADAS document.
