Scope, provenance, and terminology guidance

Three distinct states

  • Observed: a public record in this snapshot used the label.
  • Registered: WordPress offers the term in that field. Species, program, and external-resource registries were checked October 16; the original five fields retain their dated usage basis.
  • Semantically verified: evidence supports the label for the content and any applicable ontology mapping.

How to assess a candidate

Use NCIt for disease concepts and UBERON for primary disease sites. Use the NCI Imaging Submission Model and the GDC schema as terminology guides for category, data type, and file type. Their values do not automatically become valid WordPress options.

Historical diagnosis and anatomy mappings come from the source picklist workbook. API checks confirm whether a code exists and is active; they do not approve its meaning for a TCIA label. Confirmed decisions override historical candidates. Reference links were checked against current definitions and synonyms. Reference scope and supporting notes are retained in the spreadsheet export; a reference does not approve its application to every dataset. Non-Cancer remains an unlinked TCIA filter, paired with specific labels where supported.

Export spreadsheet downloads all eight fields, including the displayed ontology links and confirmed decisions, plus historical evidence for audit purposes. Search and filters do not limit the export.

Confirmed labeling decisions

Non-Cancer: retain the broad filter and add supported specific disease or healthy-cohort labels. Nonmalignant disease does not automatically mean Healthy Subject.

Phantoms: represent phantom identity in species. Retain supported simulated disease context: VICTRE keeps Breast Cancer + Non-Cancer with Computational Phantom species. Use Not Applicable where disease/site does not apply.

Broad groups: Gastroesophageal Cancer covers stomach and esophageal cancers; Neuroendocrine Tumors (all sites) includes tumors and carcinomas. Gastroesophageal Cancer links to both gastric and esophageal malignancy concepts; the neuroendocrine umbrella links to Neuroendocrine Neoplasm.

Ewing/pPNET: replace the combined label with separate Ewing Sarcoma and Peripheral Primitive Neuroectodermal Tumor labels according to the diagnoses actually represented. The historical row remains visible until usage is refreshed.
